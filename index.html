18.66px / 24px equivalent — WCAG "large text" threshold

Ratios follow the WCAG 2.1 relative-luminance formula, from 1:1 (no contrast) to 21:1 (black on white). "Large text" means 18.66px+ bold or 24px+ regular. AA is the baseline most sites target; AAA is the stricter tier for enhanced accessibility.
