Student Help Portal - FAQ

The college working hours are generally from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM from Monday to Friday.
Students can apply for examinations through the college examination portal before the announced deadline.
Students can check their attendance through the student portal using their college login credentials.
Students can contact the department office or email the department using the official college contact details.
Study materials can be obtained from the department portal, learning management system, or respective subject teachers.
